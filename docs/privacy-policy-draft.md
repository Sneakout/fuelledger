# FuelNerve Privacy Policy

**Draft for review — not yet published or effective**
Prepared: 19 November 2026

FuelNerve helps fuel stations manage shifts, sales, inventory, purchases, customer accounts and business reporting. This policy explains how information is handled when you use FuelNerve, including its web application, owner application, Intelligence features and WhatsApp updates.

## 1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers account holders, staff, people who request a demo or support, and people whose information is provided to FuelNerve by a participating fuel station, such as customers and suppliers.

Fuel station owners decide which staff and customer records to enter and which communications to request. FuelNerve processes those business records to provide the service. We also handle account, service administration, security and support information for operating FuelNerve.

If a fuel station entered your information, contact that fuel station about its business records and use of your information. You may also contact FuelNerve using the contact details below.

## 2. Information we handle

- **Account and access information:** name, email address, mobile number, role, fuel station access, sign-in and session information. Passwords are stored as hashes. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive information needed to identify and authenticate your account, including your Google account email and name.
- **Fuel station records:** station details, products and prices, staff assignments, shifts, nozzle readings, tank dips and density, stock movements, purchases, supplier payments, sales, collections, expenses, customer credit and repayments, and associated accounting records.
- **Customer and supplier information:** names, contact details, account identifiers, vehicle details where entered, payment terms, outstanding balances and transaction history.
- **Documents and requests:** uploaded invoices, DSRs, photos, PDFs and other attachments, extracted readings, questions submitted to Ask FuelNerve, feedback and support requests.
- **Communication information:** WhatsApp destinations, notification preferences, consent records, reminder approvals, message content, provider message identifiers, delivery statuses, failures and suppression reasons. Where push notifications are enabled, we handle the device registration information needed to deliver them.
- **Technical and audit information:** request and error logs, device or browser information, network information such as IP addresses, timestamps, and records of changes and approvals.

Please provide only information needed for operating your fuel station. Do not upload unrelated identity documents, passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## 3. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate users, control access, maintain business records, calculate stock and financial summaries, process document imports, provide reports and Intelligence features, send requested notifications, respond to support requests, investigate errors and misuse, and maintain audit history.

Uploaded documents may be retained with the relevant business record so authorised users can review the source. Automatically extracted information can be inaccurate and should be checked before confirmation.

## 4. Intelligence and document processing

Some document reading takes place in your browser. Features that use server-based AI may send uploaded documents or images, extracted text, user questions, and relevant business facts to OpenAI for processing. These features are not entirely on-device.

FuelNerve uses programmed calculations for balances and uses AI to help read documents, interpret questions, explain findings and prioritise attention. Review the supporting records before acting on an AI explanation or document draft. AI processing does not itself authorise changes to your business records.

External providers process information under their applicable terms and data-handling arrangements. This policy does not promise that information sent to an external provider is immediately deleted.

## 5. WhatsApp and other notifications

When WhatsApp updates are enabled, FuelNerve shares the recipient's number and the message content with Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Messages may contain fuel station sales, collections, stock, purchases or customer balances, depending on the enabled category and recipient.

Owners choose their preferences in **Settings → WhatsApp alerts**. Customer payment reminders require recorded consent for the destination number and must meet the applicable reminder policy. Fuel station owners are responsible for ensuring that consent and contact details they provide are accurate.

You can withdraw owner WhatsApp consent in settings. For customer reminders, ask the fuel station to pause reminders or withdraw your consent. You can also contact FuelNerve. A withdrawal stops future eligible sending once processed; it cannot recall a message already sent. Delivery and consent history may be retained for auditing and investigating communication requests.

WhatsApp messages and notification previews may be visible to others who can access the recipient's phone or linked devices. Choose recipients accordingly. Meta also handles information under its own privacy terms.

## 6. Who can receive information

Information may be available to authorised users within your fuel station organisation according to their access permissions, and to FuelNerve personnel when needed for support, security or service administration.

We use service providers to operate relevant features, including **Vercel** for hosting, **Neon** for database services, **OpenAI** for configured AI features, **Meta** for WhatsApp, and **Google** when Google sign-in is selected. Providers receive information relevant to their service. Other notification services may process device delivery information when the corresponding feature is enabled.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or when necessary to investigate misuse and protect the service or people's rights. A change in ownership of the service may involve transferring records, subject to applicable obligations and appropriate notice.

## 7. Browser storage and devices

FuelNerve uses session cookies and browser storage to support sign-in, preferences, cached data, offline work and saved drafts. A historical DSR draft may include a local copy of the attached document and edited readings. Saving a browser draft does not mean the import has been submitted.

Use the available discard controls to remove unwanted drafts. Clearing site data can remove local drafts and unsynchronised work. Signing out should not be assumed to erase every locally stored document or draft. Protect shared devices and avoid leaving business records available to other users.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service and for applicable accounting, audit, dispute, security or legal purposes. The appropriate period depends on the record and the fuel station's obligations. This policy does not establish a single automatic deletion period for all records.

You may request account closure or deletion by contacting FuelNerve. We may need to verify your identity and authority, and coordinate with the fuel station that controls the business record. Some records may need to be retained or restricted rather than immediately deleted. Backup copies and provider-held records may remain for their applicable retention cycles; local browser copies require separate removal.

## 9. Your choices and requests

You can update supported profile details, manage notification preferences and ask for access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Contact the relevant fuel station for records it maintains about you, or contact FuelNerve for help. We will assess requests under applicable requirements and explain any limits that apply.

Fuel station administrators should provide appropriate notice to their staff and customers and have authority to upload their information and request communications.

## 10. Security and international processing

FuelNerve uses safeguards such as authenticated access, role and fuel station permissions, password hashing and checks on delivery callbacks. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep credentials private, limit staff access appropriately and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

Our providers may process information outside your country. The location depends on the provider and the service configuration. Do not assume that every record is stored or processed only in India.

## 11. Children

FuelNerve is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been provided inappropriately.
